# Bruxus

Bruxus is a digital second brain for a human being. It works like a brain, but it lives in the digital world: it perceives what is happening there, interprets it, and tells its human what matters, so the person can understand and act on their digital surroundings without having to watch all of it themselves.

## Purpose

Help a person perceive and interpret their surroundings programmatically. Where a human brain interprets the physical world through senses, Bruxus interprets the digital one: messages, documents, feeds, code, data, systems and events.

## Core idea

A brain does four things, and Bruxus mirrors them:

1. **Perceive**: take in signals from the digital world through connectors (sensors).
2. **Remember**: keep what was perceived in a structured, searchable memory.
3. **Interpret**: turn raw signals into meaning (what this is, why it matters, how it relates to what is already known).
4. **Inform and act**: present the interpretation to the human, and act on their behalf when asked.

See [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) for the first sketch of the system.
